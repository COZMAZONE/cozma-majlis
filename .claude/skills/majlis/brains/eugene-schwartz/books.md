# Books: Eugene Schwartz

Built from the public archive.org scan of *Breakthrough Advertising* (short quotes only) and from how others describe his other works. No book text is reproduced beyond short quotes.

## Breakthrough Advertising: How to Write Ads That Shatter Traditions and Sales Records (1966)

- **Year:** 1966, Prentice-Hall. Out of print for years; reprinted by Boardroom in small batches, then republished unchanged by Brian Kurtz (Titans Marketing) from 2016 (S026, S034).
- **Core thesis:** Copy cannot create desire; it channels mass desire that already exists onto one product. The headline is located by the market's state of awareness and sophistication; the body builds desire, identification, and belief through seven techniques.
- **Key frameworks:** Part 1 (strategy): mass desire, functional product and dominant performance, five stages of awareness, five stages of sophistication, the headline's real job, 38 ways of verbalization, three levels of creativity. See frameworks.md #1 to #10. Part 2 (techniques): desires, identifications, beliefs; intensification, identification, gradualization, redefinition, mechanization, concentration, camouflage; reinforcement, interweaving, momentum, mood. See frameworks.md #11 to #20.
- **Taught in:** S001 to S016 (chapters), S019, S028, S034, S037, S041.

## The Brilliance Breakthrough: How to Talk and Write So That People Will Never Forget You (written about 30 years after BA, per Kurtz)

- **Year:** Kurtz says it was written almost 30 years after 1966 and republished by him in 2017 (S025).
- **Core thesis:** Choose the right, concrete word before grammar; think and write in images; short images are easier to grasp; from words build images, from images sentences, from sentences whole discourses.
- **Key frameworks:** Writing in pictures and the window principle (principles.md #19 to #21); clarity guideposts used in frameworks.md #9 and #20.
- **Taught in:** S022, S025, S031, S039 (all describing or quoting it; the corpus does not include the book itself).
