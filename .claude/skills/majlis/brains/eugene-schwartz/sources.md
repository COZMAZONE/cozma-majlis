# Sources: Eugene Schwartz

42 sources. S001 to S016 are the chapters of *Breakthrough Advertising* (Prentice-Hall, 1966), read from the public scan on archive.org and cached locally one chapter per id so every quote can be checked against its chapter; only short quotes are used, and no passage of the book is reproduced. S017 to S042 are talk excerpts, tributes, interviews, and articles that quote his seminars to Rodale, Phillips, and Boardroom copywriters (early 1980s to 1994), his 33:33 timer habit, his research and copy-assembly method, and *The Brilliance Breakthrough* (as quoted by its republisher Brian Kurtz). Quotes in the brain are verbatim from these cached texts. Where a source is a secondary write-up of a talk (S017, S018, S019, S020, S021, S038), the quote is the author's transcription of Schwartz, not an official transcript; no full transcript of the 1993 Phillips "Lost Secrets" lecture or the 1994 seminar is public. Quotes attributed to Kurtz, Simpson, Bruce, or Emerson are their words, not his.
