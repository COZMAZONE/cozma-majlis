Each source is cached as plain text (`S0NN.txt`), and every quote in this brain is verbatim from that cache. Podcast transcripts are machine transcriptions (podscripts.co), so they keep caption quirks; trade-press items mix his direct quotes with the reporter's paraphrase, and only his quoted words are used as quotes here.
