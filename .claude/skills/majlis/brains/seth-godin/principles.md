# Principles: Seth Godin

12 decision rules, clustered across sources. Confidence: high = 3+ sources, medium = 2, low = 1. Quotes are verbatim from the fetched text.

## Audience

**1. Decide who it's for. Engaging everyone delights no one.**
- "When you seek to engage with everyone, you rarely delight anyone." (S002)
- "Not everyone, but someone." (S001)
- Sources: S001, S002, S003, S005. Confidence: high.

**2. Start smaller than feels safe. The smallest viable audience is a stepping-stone, not a retreat.**
- "It’s a stepping-stone, not a compromise." (S001)
- "The smallest viable audience isn’t an excuse. It’s the point." (S005)
- Sources: S001, S002, S004, S005. Confidence: high.

**3. Aiming at the mass produces the average.**
- "On the other hand, if you aim for mass (another word for average), you’ll probably create something average." (S003)
- Sources: S003, S004, S015. Confidence: high.

**4. Scale comes from spread, not from aiming wide.**
- "You get there by embracing the 1% and doing such a good job that the word spreads." (S004)
- "And then, ironically enough, the word will spread." (S002)
- Sources: S002, S003, S004, S015. Confidence: high.

## Product and story

**5. Safe is risky. Very good is invisible; remarkable spreads.**
- "The riskiest thing you can do now is be safe." (S015)
- "Cows are invisible. Cows are boring." (S015)
- Sources: S015 (and the Purple Cow book it summarizes). Confidence: low (one source, but it is his central idea).

**6. Remarkable is judged by the customer you chose, not by you.**
- No verbatim line in the brain; the TED talk frames it as finding the people who care obsessively and making something they talk about (S015), and the SVA posts frame it as delighting a chosen group (S001, S003).
- Sources: S001, S003, S015. Confidence: medium.

**7. A brand is a kept promise; design only marks it.**
- "Design is essential but design is not brand." (S007)
- "Expectation is in the eye of the beholder, but expectation is often enhanced and hyped by the marketer hoping for a quick win." (S008)
- Sources: S007, S008. Confidence: medium.

## Permission and trust

**8. Attention is paid to you; spend it like money you cannot get back.**
- "Attention becomes an important asset, something to be valued, not wasted." (S006)
- Sources: S006, S015. Confidence: medium.

**9. Keep exactly the promise you made to get permission.**
- "But the promise is the promise until both sides agree to change it." (S006)
- "The best shortcut, in this case, is no shortcut at all." (S006)
- Sources: S006. Confidence: low.

## Price

**10. Do not compete on cheapest. Someone will always go lower, and you cut what made you worth it.**
- "Cheap is the last refuge for the marketer who can’t figure out how to be better." (S013)
- "Someone will always find a way to be cheaper or more brutal than you." (S014)
- Sources: S011, S012, S013, S014. Confidence: high.

**11. Be worth it: charge more and over-deliver.**
- "An alternative is: You’ll pay more than you hoped but get more than you paid for." (S012)
- Sources: S011, S012, S014, S017 (episode titled "Price is a story."). Confidence: high.

## Measurement

**12. Measure direct marketing ruthlessly; give brand marketing patience and consistency.**
- "Direct marketing is outbound, measured and designed to pay for itself." (S009)
- "If you can’t afford to be consistent and patient, don’t pay for brand marketing ads." (S016)
- Sources: S009, S010, S016. Confidence: high.
