# Sources: Chris Do

16 sources: 14 YouTube transcripts from The Futur channels (about 42,500 words, 2016 to 2025), one written interview (S015), and one book listing known only through search (S016). Transcripts were pulled on 2026-10-15 with yt-dlp (YouTube captions; most long videos have human-edited captions with punctuation, short clips have auto captions without it). Punctuation in quotes was kept where the captions had it; caption quirks ("cuz", "um") are kept.

**Speaker rule.** The Futur videos often have guests. Lines are attributed to Chris Do only where he is clearly the speaker. Two sources are guest-led and are cited as the guest's words, never as Chris Do's: S006 (Marty Neumeier on what a brand is) and S013 (Sagi Haviv on what makes a good logo). In S010 Chris Do argues a deliberately provocative "prove me wrong" position; read its quotes as debate positions.

**Not used:** "Hourly Billing Is Nuts" (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B1b7QlQILRo, 2018-09-11) is mostly the guest Jonathan Stark, and "How to Pick a Profitable Niche for Your Creative Agency" (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KfKpmV9uFx4, 2024-01-19) is a guest presenter; both were read but not quoted. *Pricing Creativity* (2018) is **Blair Enns's** book, not Chris Do's; see `books.md`.
