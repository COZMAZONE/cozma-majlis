# Principles: Chris Do

17 decision rules. Confidence: high = 3+ sources, medium = 2, low = 1. Quotes are verbatim from captions.

## Diagnosis and strategy

**1. Find the real problem before you solve one.**
- "How can you diagnose if you haven't asked me any questions?" (S001)
- "We need to be problem seekers." (S008)
- Sources: S001, S005, S008, S012. Confidence: high.

**2. Strategy is a goal and a path to it.**
- "Strategy is basically you trying to figure out what the goal is and then figuring out how to get to that goal." (S005)
- Sources: S001, S005. Confidence: medium.

**3. Ask "why" until it reaches the business.**
- "why is this important to you what business problem does this solve how does this improve your bottom line" (S004)
- "What problem does this solve for $1,000?" (S009)
- Sources: S004, S009, S001. Confidence: high.

**4. Look for the business problem, not your favourite deliverable.**
- "I'm looking for a person with a business problem that sometimes a logo's the answer." (S008)
- Sources: S008. Confidence: low.

## Value and money

**5. Sell and buy the outcome, not the thing.**
- "Your client desires something beyond the thing that you make. But what we almost always do is sell the thing that we make." (S008)
- Sources: S001, S008. Confidence: medium.

**6. Quantify value in money.**
- "So every time you can dollarize the value of what it is that you do, to put it in real terms, it's an easy sell." (S008)
- "if we can't quantify it it becomes very difficult for us to communicate to someone who thinks in a quantifiable" (S004)
- Sources: S001, S004, S008. Confidence: high.

**7. Value is the buyer's judgment; price is the seller's decision.**
- "the buyer determines value the seller determines price" (S004)
- "people tend to spend money on what they value." (S009)
- Sources: S004, S009. Confidence: medium.

**8. Never price by the hour.**
- "So, charging by the hour punishes me for being good." (S002)
- "a freelancer is a person who sells their time for money" (S004)
- Sources: S002, S003, S004. Confidence: high.

**9. Budget in proportion to the stakes.**
- "do not spend a small effort for a big result. And don't spend a big effort for a small result." (S001)
- "So there is a direct correlation to size of problem in the client's mind, not yours." (S008)
- Sources: S001, S002, S008, S009. Confidence: high.

**10. Don't fix the wrong client and wrong service with a lower price.**
- Paraphrase (S008): when we chase the wrong problem with the wrong client and the wrong service, the reflex is to offer to do it for less, which fixes nothing.
- Sources: S008, S002. Confidence: medium.

**11. Confidence comes from having more opportunities than capacity.**
- "the goal in running any business is to have more opportunities than capacity" (S004)
- "the more discriminating you are what are you doing you're communicating scarcity" (S004)
- Sources: S004, S002. Confidence: medium.

## Trust and risk

**12. Be the least risky option.**
- "Clients don't choose the best option. They choose the least risky option." (S002)
- "You need to appear as the least risky thing." (S002)
- Sources: S002. Confidence: low.

## Brand and design

**13. The logo is a symbol; the brand is the whole experience.**
- "if you're not doing strategy positioning product development user experience design customer service design you my friend are not doing branding you're creating symbols" (S010)
- "a logo is a very useful tool for a business but it's not the brand it's a symbol for the brand" (S006, Marty Neumeier)
- Sources: S006, S010, S011. Confidence: high.

**14. Aesthetics still matter, and simplicity passes the test.**
- "if anything the simpler your logo is the easier it is to pass this test" (S011)
- "what is the purpose of any marketing or design effort it's to bring in new customers and to keep the existing customers really happy" (S011)
- Sources: S010, S011, S013. Confidence: high.

**15. A logo starts a long story; don't change it lightly.**
- "ideally when you create a logo it begins a story that you don't want to change" (S011)
- Sources: S011. Confidence: low.

## Focus and craft

**16. Pick a lane and show only your best.**
- "if we exercise a little discipline we pick a lane we can probably do really well for ourselves" (S007)
- "don't put anything you're not super proud of and be merciless be vigilant in what you keep" (S007)
- Sources: S007, S012. Confidence: medium.

**17. Do less, but better; master the fundamentals.**
- "I think the way to achieve Excellence is to do less but to do better" (S012)
- "I think you should try to master something first before you move on" (S012)
- Sources: S012, S007. Confidence: medium.
