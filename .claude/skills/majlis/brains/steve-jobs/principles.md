# Principles: Steve Jobs

17 decision rules. Confidence: high = 3+ sources, medium = 2, low = 1. Quotes are verbatim; see `sources.md` for which ones come through reproductions.

## Customer and product

**1. Start from the customer experience, not the technology.**
- "you've got to start with the customer experience and work backwards to the technology." (S004)
- "what incredible benefits can we give to the customer? Where can we take the customer?" (S005)
- Sources: S003, S004, S005. Confidence: high (one event, three independent transcriptions).

**2. Customers judge results, not effort.**
- "The customers aren't going to measure us on how people tried or how hungry they were. They're going to measure us on what they see." (S003)
- Sources: S003. Confidence: low.

**3. Make a few really great products.**
- "And that strategy revolves around one fundamental concept, which is to make some really great products." (S003)
- Sources: S003, S007. Confidence: medium.

**4. Be much better; different only if needed.**
- "If we can be much better without being different, that would be fine with me." (S003)
- "I want to be much better. I don't care about being different." (S003)
- Sources: S003. Confidence: low.

**5. Don't reinvent the world when the job is to deliver what already works.**
- "It's to take something that we know exists already but hardly anybody's got it, and get it out to them." (S003)
- Sources: S003. Confidence: low.

## Focus

**6. Focus is saying no, many times.**
- "And it comes from saying no to 1,000 things to make sure we don't get on the wrong track or try to do too much." (S007)
- "When you say no, you piss off people." (S003)
- Sources: S003, S004, S007, S012 (Markkula's focus point). Confidence: high.

**7. Kill good things that don't fit.**
- "I think it was great technology but it didn't fit." (S003)
- Sources: S003, S004. Confidence: medium.

## Design and taste

**8. Design is how it works, not a veneer.**
- "Some people think design means how it looks. But of course, if you dig deeper, it's really how it works." (S002)
- "Most people make the mistake of thinking design is what it looks like," (S008)
- Sources: S002, S008. Confidence: medium.

**9. To design something well, understand it deeply.**
- "To design something really well, you have to get it. You have to really grok what it's all about." (S002)
- "It takes a passionate commitment to really thoroughly understand something, chew it up, not just quickly swallow it." (S002)
- Sources: S002. Confidence: low.

**10. Simple is hard and worth it.**
- "Simple can be harder than complex. You have to work hard to get your thinking clean to make it simple." (S006)
- "One of the major reasons for the Web's proliferation so far is its simplicity." (S002)
- Sources: S002, S006. Confidence: medium.

**11. Taste matters, and it comes from culture and broad experience.**
- "The only problem with Microsoft is they just have no taste. They have absolutely no taste." (S009)
- "The broader one's understanding of the human experience, the better design we will have." (S002)
- "It was the first computer with beautiful typography." (S001)
- Sources: S001, S002, S009. Confidence: high.

**12. Creativity is connecting experiences.**
- "Creativity is just connecting things." (S002)
- "That's because they were able to connect experiences they've had and synthesize new things." (S002)
- Sources: S001, S002. Confidence: medium.

**13. Don't design by focus group; show people.**
- "A lot of times, people don't know what they want until you show it to them." (S006)
- Sources: S006, S010. Confidence: medium.

## Craft and people

**14. Craftsmanship is the gap between a good idea and a good product.**
- "They have no conception of the craftsmanship that's required, to take a good idea and turn it into a good product." (S011)
- Sources: S001 (calligraphy into the Mac), S011. Confidence: medium.

**15. When product people lose control to sales and marketing, products rot.**
- "The product sensibility, and the product genius that brought them to that monopolistic position, gets rotted out by people running these companies who have no conception of a good product versus a bad product," (S011)
- Sources: S011. Confidence: low.

**16. Work only with great people and agencies.**
- "They just need to be great. The results need to be great." (S003)
- Sources: S003, S011. Confidence: medium.

## Courage

**17. Follow your own judgment; don't live by other people's thinking.**
- "Don't be trapped by dogma, which is living with the results of other people's thinking." (S001)
- "Don't let the noise of others' opinions drown out your own inner voice." (S001)
- Sources: S001. Confidence: low (one source, but his most-cited speech).
