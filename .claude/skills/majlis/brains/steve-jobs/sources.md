# Sources: Steve Jobs

14 sources, all fetched and read on 2026-10-15, about 120,000 words of page text. Two are full primary texts (S001 Stanford 2005, S002 Wired 1996), one is a full fan transcript of a recorded session (S003 WWDC 1997), and the rest are interviews and documents known through reputable reproductions. Jobs wrote no books; his public thought lives in talks, keynotes, and interviews.

**Read the "Fetched via" column.** Several original articles (BusinessWeek 1998 and 2004, The New York Times Magazine 2003, Triumph of the Nerds, Playboy 1985, Fortune 2000, Time 1999) were paywalled or blocked. Their quoted lines were taken from reproductions that cite the original, and are well documented across many outlets, but the wording was not checked against the original page. Treat those quotes as reliable but secondary, and never extend them beyond the quoted words.

**S012 is not Jobs.** The 1977 "Apple Marketing Philosophy" memo is credited to Mike Markkula (some accounts say Jobs and Markkula). Quote it as Markkula's memo that shaped early Apple, never as Jobs's words.
