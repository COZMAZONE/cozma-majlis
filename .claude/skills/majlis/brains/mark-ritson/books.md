# Books and courses: Mark Ritson

Ritson's main public body of work is his weekly column (Marketing Week since about 2010, The Drum more recently) and his Mini MBA courses. No book text or paid course material is used in this brain.

## Columns (Marketing Week, The Drum)

- **Core thesis across them:** Marketing has lost its strategic core to tactics and fads; return to training, research, segmentation, targeting, positioning, objectives, and then tactics. Do long and short. Hold a tight position. Never discount. Manage for profit.
- **In this brain:** S001, S002, S003, S005, S006, S009, S010, S011.

## Mini MBA in Marketing and Mini MBA in Brand Management (online courses)

- **What is public:** free webinars and summaries on minimba.com. "Bothism" (doing both brand and performance, targeting and mass, distinctiveness and differentiation) is described as a persistent theme of the courses (S013).
- **In this brain:** S013 (summary of his free "Proper Advertising: The Five Steps" webinar). Paid modules not used.

## Talks and debates

- **Festival of Marketing:** 2017 debate with Byron Sharp (S004); a 10-step talk on advertising effectiveness (S012).
- **ADMA Global Forum:** differentiation and distinctiveness (S008).
- **Diagnosis, Strategy, Tactics:** his teaching model, as described by others (S014).

## Influences he credits

- Les Binet and Peter Field, The Long and the Short of It (he calls it more important than fans realise, S013).
- Byron Sharp and the Ehrenberg-Bass Institute for salience, distinctive assets, and mental and physical availability, while rejecting their dismissal of differentiation and targeting (S003, S008).
