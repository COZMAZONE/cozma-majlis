# Principles: Mark Ritson

12 decision rules. Confidence: high = 3+ sources, medium = 2, low = 1. Quotes are verbatim from the fetched text.

## Process

**1. Strategy before tactics. Pick the battle before the weapon.**
- "They already have their crossbow drawn with no clue where, who or what they are attacking." (S001)
- "My advice would be" to "try and get strategy first before you start talking about TV, Facebook or anything." (S004, two fragments of one sentence)
- Sources: S001, S004, S012, S014. Confidence: high.

**2. Research the market; never assume. You are not the customer.**
- "You cannot assume anything about your consumer, because invariably your assumptions are based on conjecture." (S006)
- "If [marketers] had been properly trained, the first thing they would have been trained on is market orientation, which means you are not the consumer," (S007)
- Sources: S006, S007, S002. Confidence: high.

**3. Segment with data, then target.**
- "What is needed for proper segmentation is, of course, a bit of data." (S002)
- Sources: S001, S002, S004. Confidence: high.

## Brand

**4. Mass reach and targeting are not rivals.**
- "It’s not A against B, it’s A and B versus just B," (S004)
- Sources: S004, S003. Confidence: medium.

**5. Position on three words or fewer, chosen from research and different from rivals.**
- "Any more than that and the probability of achieving any kind of impact on the market turns almost immediately to zero." (S005)
- "Choose one, two, three things and focus on it for a long time" (S008)
- Sources: S005, S008, S009. Confidence: high.

**6. You need distinctiveness and differentiation.**
- "Distinctiveness alone is not enough." (S008)
- Sources: S008, S009, S012. Confidence: high.

**7. Consistency wins; do not change what works because you are bored.**
- "In order for you to drive savings, the most important thing for your brand [...] is to look like yourself," (S012, bracket as published)
- Good advertising does not wear out (S012, paraphrase); Tourism Australia ran the same campaign again on purpose (S008).
- Sources: S008, S012. Confidence: medium.

## Advertising

**8. Do long and short, in separate ads, and spend more on the long than you think.**
- "When you do that calculator, what you’re all going to discover, to your amazement, is you’re not spending enough money on long you are overspending on short," (S012)
- "It’s And, not Versus" (S013)
- Sources: S012, S013, S004. Confidence: high.

**9. Budget from objectives and returns, not as a percentage of revenue.**
- "If I’ve already worked out how much money I’m going to make next year, what’s the point of you?" (S012)
- Sources: S012. Confidence: low.

**10. Pre-test creative; it is cheap now.**
- "You’re a muppet not to do it frankly." (S012)
- Sources: S012. Confidence: low.

## Price and profit

**11. Do not discount. The damage lives in the gap between your price and the deal.**
- "I attack price promotions because they truly are the crack cocaine of promotional activity." (S009)
- "The spike was never growth. It was a loan." (S011)
- Sources: S009, S010, S011. Confidence: high.

**12. Manage for profit, not sales volume.**
- "Profits are the lifeblood of every company." (S009)
- "Yes, I know discounting drives up revenue. But who cares?" (S010)
- Sources: S009, S010, S011. Confidence: high.
