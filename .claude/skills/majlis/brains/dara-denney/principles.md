# Principles: Dara Denney

22 decision rules, clustered across sources. Confidence: high = 3+ sources, medium = 2, low = 1. Quotes are verbatim from captions or published transcripts.

## What wins

**1. Creative is the biggest lever on paid social. When results are bad, assume a creative problem first.**
- "nine times out of 10 you likely have a creative problem on your hands especially if you're at those earlier stages" (S009)
- "your creative is going to be your biggest lever on the platform" (S009)
- Sources: 4 (S003, S009, S011, S029). Confidence: high.

**2. Messaging and the creator matter more than format. Format is how an idea shows up; the idea and the person carry it.**
- "Your creator and the talent that you work with is more important candidly than the script, the arbitrary hooks that you're making." (S004)
- "how much more the messaging impacts the creative as opposed to the format or the visuals" (S019)
- Sources: 5 (S004, S005, S010, S019, S030). Confidence: high.

**3. Be specific. Exact prices, ages, and details stick; vague claims blend in.**
- "I just think that the more specific you can be, the more that you're going to stick out in the minds of your users and differentiate yourself from the competition." (S001)
- Sources: 3 (S001, S022, S024). Confidence: high.

**4. Use the customer's words, not the brand's. Mine reviews, comments, and Reddit for language and hooks.**
- "we did use language that was pulled again directly from website reviews" (S001)
- "the best way to find these investments is to upload a CSV of all your customer reviews and search for the failed attempts" (S014)
- Sources: 6 (S001, S002, S003, S004, S014, S020). Confidence: high.

**5. The most scalable message is the one that speaks to the widest group, usually higher up the funnel.**
- "what ultimately ended up winning for us was the one that had the most scalable messaging." (S005)
- "if you can get creative winners from this problem aware area you are going to have a better ability to scale these ads" (S024)
- Sources: 5 (S005, S010, S014, S017, S024). Confidence: high.

**6. Curiosity holds attention. Do not give away the plot in the first line.**
- "You like gave away the plot, right?" (S002)
- "you're going to make your customer's brains crave resolution" (S002)
- Sources: 4 (S002, S013, S014, S015). Confidence: high.

## Testing

**7. Every test needs a reason. If you will not learn something, do not run it.**
- "if you don't have a reason for testing a creative and if you are not going to learn something from testing it, then you probably shouldn't be testing it." (S003)
- Sources: 2 (S003, S006). Confidence: medium.

**8. Take big swings. Small tweaks buy small results; a few bold tests a month win.**
- "the brands that I see taking a few big swings every single month, those are the ones that tend to win more often than not." (S001)
- Sources: 5 (S001, S009, S012, S013, S019). Confidence: high.

**9. Most tests lose. Plan for it; winners are rare and that is normal.**
- "anywhere from 70 to 90% of the creatives and messaging and new angles you're going to test, they're not going to be bangers." (S006)
- "But in terms of absolute banger winner, unicorn ads kind of rare." (S001)
- Sources: 3 (S001, S006, S008). Confidence: high.

**10. Iterate winners until they die; let losers die.**
- "anything that's like a top performer or unicorn ad I'm going to iterate that thing until it dies" (S012)
- "9 out of 10 times do not make iterations on losing creative it's losing for a reason" (S019)
- Sources: 3 (S012, S019, S030). Confidence: high.

**11. Keep testing away from what already works, and test on broad.**
- "I can't tell you how many times I've added a creative test to an otherwise performing campaign and I saw performance tank" (S009)
- "if it's going to work on broad, it's going to work on all your other audiences." (S001)
- Sources: 4 (S001, S009, S011, S021). Confidence: high.

**12. Find the message cheaply, then spend on video and production.**
- "however wouldn't start testing a ton of new video until you start learning what type of messaging is actually converting your customers" (S009)
- "they will rapidly test a lot of messaging points using images and then take those messaging learnings and apply those to their UGC content" (S001)
- Sources: 4 (S001, S009, S022, S030). Confidence: high.

## Reading results

**13. Judge on spend and results; everything else only tells the story.**
- "Now, your primary KPIs are going to tell you which creatives worked and which ones didn't. That is all." (S004)
- "these four metrics are essentially going to show you if your campaign is working and specifically what is working everything else is going to tell you why" (S017)
- Sources: 3 (S004, S017, S020). Confidence: high.

**14. Data says what won, not why. Get comfortable making the subjective call.**
- "And the data isn't always going to reveal why a creative worked." (S001)
- "the analysis process does not just stop at the data." (S004)
- Sources: 4 (S001, S004, S017, S030). Confidence: high.

**15. Analyze before you optimize.**
- "people try to optimize and analyze at the same time but really the analysis needs to be done first" (S017)
- Sources: 1 (S017). Confidence: low.

**16. Respect what the algorithm chose. Forcing spend onto "more efficient" small ads usually fails.**
- Paraphrase: she has tried throttling spend to ads with better CPA but little spend, and they typically do not hold up; Meta calls this the breakdown effect (S017).
- Sources: 2 (S004, S017). Confidence: medium.

## Diversity and the system

**17. Diversity beats volume. Give the system genuinely different ads for different people.**
- "Volume is not exactly the answer. It's much more about your creative efficiency and your velocity." (S011)
- "When it comes to Andromeda, sure, make sure you have visual diversity and focus less on variations." (S007)
- Sources: 6 (S002, S007, S010, S011, S012, S029). Confidence: high.

**18. Every ad should be built for a named persona.**
- "Which is why every time you're analyzing creative and you're looking at the format, the creator, the messaging, and the imagery, really think about, okay, who are we targeting with this specific creative?" (S004)
- Sources: 5 (S002, S004, S010, S028, S030). Confidence: high.

**19. Fix the economics before the ads. A low order value cannot be out-created.**
- "that AOV is actually too low to be scalable on meta ads." (S021)
- "being able to increase your AOV so you can actually spend more to acquire your customers is going to be a crucial part of your success moving forward in this new ecosystem." (S011)
- Sources: 3 (S009, S011, S021). Confidence: high.

**20. Paid and organic are converging. Organic content is your research lab and your feed.**
- "The gap between organic and paid content in my eyes is really starting to close as we move into 2026." (S011)
- "creator content is essentially just platform content." (S020)
- Sources: 5 (S010, S011, S020, S029, S030). Confidence: high.

## Team and process

**21. When strategists underperform, suspect the process first.**
- "nine out of 10 times when I talk with brands and I talk with agencies and they think it's something about the creative strategist or the creative strategist isn't doing the right work, it's actually a failure of process." (S002)
- Sources: 2 (S002, S007). Confidence: medium.

**22. Someone must own taste and say "this is a bad ad."**
- "if you don't have someone that's owning that taste, it's going to be a struggle." (S002)
- Sources: 2 (S002, S030). Confidence: medium.
