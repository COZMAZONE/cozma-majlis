# Sources: Dara Denney

30 public sources, 2022 to 2026: 22 videos from her own YouTube channel (auto captions, pulled as text), 1 podcast interview with a published transcript, 5 Motion talks with published speaker-tagged transcripts, 1 Motion article that quotes her, and 1 third-party recap (Foreplay). About 85,000 words of her own speech once page chrome and host lines are removed.

**How thin is this?** Thinner than the book authors on the council. She has no book; her thinking lives in weekly YouTube videos, talks, and a paid course (not used). Every quote below appears verbatim in a source that was fetched and read for this brain. Auto captions have no punctuation, so punctuation and capitals were added during extraction; words were not changed. Caption errors (misheard words) were never quoted. Her newsletter, LinkedIn, and X posts were not reliably indexed and are **not** used. Topics with little or no source material are marked "No recorded position" in positions.md.

**Scope warning.** Her corpus is Meta-first (Facebook and Instagram), US direct-to-consumer brands (beauty, supplements, apparel, food), and agency budgets from about $5K to $5M a month. She says almost nothing about Snapchat, Arabic-speaking markets, or cash-on-delivery stores. Applying her to a Saudi store is the seat's reasoning, not her words, and is labelled as such.

Affiliations change across sources: Thesis agency (2022 to 2024, Senior Director of Performance Creative), independent consultant and YouTuber, Chief Evangelist at Motion (2025 to 2026), and partner at Point Guard Media (PGM) with Miguel (2025 to 2026). Several sources carry sponsor reads (Storyblocks, Particl, Motion, Modash); claims inside sponsor reads are not used as her positions.
