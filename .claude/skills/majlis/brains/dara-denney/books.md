# Books: Dara Denney

She has written no book. Her teaching lives on her YouTube channel (weekly long-form videos since about 2019, past 100K subscribers in 2025, S002, S004), conference talks (Motion's Creative Strategy Summit and Creative Trends events), and a paid course. This file records the course and the outside books she builds on, as she describes them in public. No book or course text is reproduced.

## The Performance Creative Master Course (her course)

- **What it is:** Her paid course on performance creative and creative strategy, first launched in mid-2023 with a section on working with creators and making UGC (S023), and being redone for early 2026 (S004).
- **What it covers, from public mentions:** the creative strategist role, research, human desires (she says she first taught the 16 desires there, S019), "educational knowledge shares" as a format (S020), testing, and analysis.
- **Not used:** the course itself is paid and was not read. Cite her videos and talks, not the course.

## Outside books and thinkers she teaches from

- **David Ogilvy.** The "big idea" behind every ad and the feature-callout static. She says Ogilvy's print-era research found callouts helped people retain product information, which is why the format still works (S001), and she trains strategists to state each ad's big idea in one line (S030).
- **Steven Reiss, *Who Am I?*** Source of the 16 basic human desires she uses as a messaging menu (S025, S019).
- ***Cashvertising* (Drew Eric Whitman).** She names its "Life Force 8" and secondary motivators as a related school of advertising psychology (S010).
- **Eugene Schwartz's awareness levels.** She uses the five stages from unaware to most aware as an analysis lens; Motion's notes on her talks credit the Schwartz lineage, though she rarely names him (S002, S003, S024).
- **Peers she cites by name:** Barry Hott (founder ads, ugly ads), Sarah Levinger (format selection is not a strategy), her business partner Miguel (Viral Ads SOP, one-campaign setup), Cody Plofker of Jones Road (founder in the car, bigger influencers) (S005, S009, S014, S022, S028, S030).

## How to use this file

When the council needs the classic version of an idea (big idea, awareness, desires), the matching seats on the creative council (Ogilvy, Schwartz) own it. This seat uses those ideas the way she does: as tools for briefing, testing, and reading paid-social results.
