# Principles: David Ogilvy

28 decision rules, clustered across sources. Confidence: high = 3+ sources, medium = 2, low = 1. Quotes are verbatim; video lines keep caption spelling.

## Purpose

**1. Advertising exists to sell. Judge it by sales, not by applause.**
- "We sell – or else." (S001)
- "I want you to find it so interesting that you buy the product." (S017)
- Sources: 6 (S001, S006, S012, S014, S017, S022). Confidence: high.

**2. Advertising is information, not entertainment or art.**
- "I do not regard advertising as entertainment or an art form, but as a medium of information." (S014)
- "The more informative your advertising, the more persuasive it will be." (S027)
- Sources: 4 (S014, S016, S027, S006). Confidence: high.

**3. Hide the craft. A good ad points at the product, not at itself.**
- "A good advertisement is one which sells the product without drawing attention to itself." (S036)
- "most of the campaigns which produce results never win awards because they don't draw attention to themselves" (S001)
- Sources: 4 (S001, S027, S017, S036). Confidence: high.

## Research and knowledge

**4. Research before you write. Knowledge beats instinct.**
- "We prefer the discipline of knowledge to the anarchy of ignorance." (S001)
- "Why should a manufacturer bet his money – perhaps the future of his company – on your instinct?" (S001)
- Sources: 8 (S001, S004, S005, S007, S008, S014, S024, S025). Confidence: high.

**5. Use research to light the way, not to defend a decision.**
- "Research is often misused by agencies and their clients." (S024)
- "We all have a tendency to use research as a drunkard uses a lamppost - for support, but not for illumination" (S016)
- Sources: 5 (S002, S007, S008, S016, S024). Confidence: high.

**6. Brand image calls need judgment; research cannot predict the long run.**
- "we've got to use judgment and judgment is an unfashionable word today in advertising" (S008)
- Sources: 2 (S002, S008). Confidence: medium.

**7. Never stop testing.**
- "Never stop testing, and your advertising will never stop improving." (S001)
- "If you pretest your product with consumers, and pretest your advertising, you will do well in the marketplace." (S013)
- Sources: 6 (S001, S004, S007, S013, S015, S016). Confidence: high.

## Strategy

**8. Positioning first, then the ad; but positioning alone does not sell.**
- "If nobody reads your advertisement or looks at your commercial, it doesn't do you much good to have the right positioning." (S001)
- "you can't save souls in an empty church" (S007)
- Sources: 5 (S001, S007, S008, S019, S029). Confidence: high.

**9. One promise. Most campaigns try to say too much.**
- "Most campaigns are too complicated." (S015)
- Sources: 4 (S001, S007, S015, S018). Confidence: high.

**10. Without a big idea, nothing else matters.**
- "You will never win fame and fortune unless you invent big ideas." (S014)
- "if you have a truly big idea the wrong technique won't kill it" (S007)
- Sources: 8 (S001, S007, S008, S010, S011, S014, S015, S029). Confidence: high.

**11. Be different from the category, not a better copy of it.**
- "Isn't the beginning of greatness in advertising to be different, and the beginning of failure to be the same?" (S002)
- "In general, study the methods of your competitors and do the exact opposite." (S018)
- Sources: 3 (S002, S007, S018). Confidence: high.

## The buyer

**12. Respect the buyer's intelligence. Give reasons and facts.**
- "The consumer isn't a moron; she is your wife." (S001)
- "You insult her intelligence if you assume that a mere slogan and a few vapid adjectives will persuade her to buy anything." (S001)
- Sources: 7 (S001, S005, S007, S008, S012, S013, S017). Confidence: high.

**13. Never be a bore.**
- "You cannot bore people into buying your product; you can only interest them in buying it." (S001)
- "The worst fault a salesman can commit is to be a bore." (S018)
- Sources: 6 (S001, S007, S010, S012, S018, S025). Confidence: high.

**14. Talk the buyer's language, not yours.**
- "It is a mistake to use highfalutin language when you advertise to uneducated people." (S001)
- "a lot of consumers are not as sophisticated as you are" (S007)
- Sources: 4 (S001, S003, S007, S015). Confidence: high.

## Honesty

**15. Honest ads for good products. If the product is weak, fix the product.**
- "Good products can be sold by honest advertising." (S013)
- "Can advertising foist an inferior product on the consumer? Bitter experience has taught me that it cannot." (S013)
- Sources: 5 (S001, S005, S013, S016, S007). Confidence: high.

**16. Persuasion is moral only if honest and respectful of beliefs.**
- "provided you do it honestly and decently without offending anybody's susceptibilities, including religious susceptibilities" (S005)
- "I abhor advertising that is blatant, dull, or dishonest." (S001)
- Sources: 3 (S001, S005, S007). Confidence: high.

**17. No superlatives, no mud-slinging at competitors.**
- "Superlatives belong to the marketplace and have no place in a serious advertisement" (S018)
- "On no account sling mud" (S018)
- Sources: 2 (S018, S021). Confidence: medium.

## Craft

**18. The headline is most of the ad.**
- "When you have written your headline, you have spent eighty cents out of your dollar." (S013)
- "unless your headline sells your product, you have wasted 90 percent of your money" (S030)
- Sources: 7 (S004, S007, S013, S015, S030, S035, S006). Confidence: high.

**19. Specific beats general; long beats short when the buyer must decide.**
- "Every word in the copy must count." (S018)
- "the more you tell generally the more you sell" (S008)
- Sources: 7 (S006, S007, S008, S018, S021, S027, S034). Confidence: high.

**20. Show it. What the eye sees beats what the ear hears.**
- "What you show is more important than what you say." (S001)
- Sources: 3 (S001, S007, S014). Confidence: high.

**21. Content over form. Taste helps; artiness does not.**
- "What really decides consumers to buy or not buy is the content of your advertising, not its form." (S001)
- "Some manufacturers illustrate their advertisements with abstract paintings." (S013)
- Sources: 4 (S001, S013, S016, S009). Confidence: high.

**22. Committees can criticize, but they cannot create.**
- "committees can criticize but they cannot create" (S007)
- Note his own exception: the Maxwell House commercial was made by three people, "which just proves that there are no rules committees can create" (S008).
- Sources: 4 (S001, S007, S008, S012). Confidence: high.

## Brand and consistency

**23. Every ad builds or erodes the brand image.**
- "Every advertisement must contribute to the complex symbol which is the brand image." (S001)
- "Any damn fool can put on a deal, but it takes genius, faith and perseverance to create a brand." (S036)
- Sources: 5 (S001, S002, S008, S031, S036). Confidence: high.

**24. Stick with a winning idea for years; boredom is not data.**
- "Scores of great advertisements have been discarded before they have begun to pay off." (S001)
- "It is tragically easy to be stampeded into change." (S001)
- Sources: 5 (S001, S002, S007, S008, S018). Confidence: high.

## Ambition and process

**25. Aim for the best ever in the category. Don't bunt.**
- "Compete with the immortals." (S011)
- "it's the lack of ambition which cripples most people" (S008)
- Sources: 4 (S001, S007, S008, S011). Confidence: high.

**26. Write many, edit hard, admit mistakes early.**
- "I am terrified of producing a lousy advertisement." (S004)
- "It is important to admit your mistakes, and to do so before you are charged with them." (S001)
- Sources: 4 (S001, S003, S004, S009). Confidence: high.

## People, faces, and proof

**27. Real testimony persuades; paid fame does not.**
- "The celebrity commercials sell us celebrities, but not the product." (S005)
- "everybody knows the consumer's been bought and that doesn't make them very credible" (S009)
- Applied: real buyers, real experts, or a real company figure (Commander Whitehead) over hired stars. A recurring real character can carry a brand for decades.
- Sources: 7 (S005, S007, S009, S014, S017, S026, S032). Confidence: high.

**28. Measure what you can; distrust what you cannot.**
- "on your side of the chasm I see knowledge and reality on the other side of the chasm I see ignorance" (S006)
- "I've been trying for some years now to build a corpus of knowledge as to what works in advertising and what doesn't work." (S005)
- Applied: every creative ships with a way to read its sales effect (code, link, split cell); judge by orders, not likes. Keep judgment for long-term image calls (rule 6).
- Sources: 5 (S005, S006, S007, S022, S024). Confidence: high.
