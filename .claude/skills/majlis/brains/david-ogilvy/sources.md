# Sources: David Ogilvy

36 sources, each cached as plain text (`S0NN.txt`). The full texts of his four books are not public, so the books enter the corpus three ways: (1) his own agency's compilation of his quotations, which tags every line with the book or memo it comes from (S001); (2) primary texts and recordings in his own words: the 1955 brand image talk, the 1955 letter on his work habits, the 1982 "How to Write" memo, the 1982 Open Mind interview, the "We Sell or Else" film, "The View From Touffou" film, a filmed 1970s conversation, and the Letterman appearance; (3) quote compilations and articles that name the book (LibQuotes, Goodreads, Wikipedia, Swiped.co, reviews). Every quote in this brain is verbatim from the cached text of the cited id, short, and never stitched. Video sources are YouTube auto captions, so they keep caption spelling; we quote only clean stretches. No book text is reproduced beyond short quotes.

Reliability: S001 to S011 are his own words (highest). S012 is a podcast that reads Confessions aloud with commentary (cite only lines clearly read from the book). S013 to S018 and S036 are compilations that name the book (medium). S019 to S035 are articles and obituaries that quote him (use for facts and stories; quotes medium to low).

## Notes on reliability

- Era wording: many lines say "she is your wife" and "men". The seat keeps quotes verbatim and applies the principle (respect the buyer's intelligence) to every buyer.
- Numbers such as five times as many people reading the headline are print readership findings from the 1950s to 1980s. Treat them as rules of thumb. The OBSOLETE headline figure differs between S001 (33%) and S013 (43%); do not quote the number.
- "If it doesn't sell, it isn't creative" appears only in S027 without a page; quote it only with that caveat.
- Not fetched: the full texts of Confessions of an Advertising Man (1963), Blood, Brains and Beer (1978), Ogilvy on Advertising (1983) and The Unpublished David Ogilvy are lending-only on archive.org (HTTP 401), and the publisher sample of The Unpublished David Ogilvy returned 403. The 1935 Aga manual is quoted only through S018.
