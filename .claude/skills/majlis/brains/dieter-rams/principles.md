# Principles: Dieter Rams

16 decision rules. Confidence: high = stated in 3+ sources or as one of the ten principles plus another source, medium = 2, low = 1. Quotes are verbatim.

## Use

**1. A product exists to be used; design starts and ends with use.**
- "A product is bought to be used." (S001)
- "Functionality must be at the centre of good design." (S003)
- "Design must always be useful, otherwise there is no design." (S006)
- Sources: S001, S003, S005, S006. Confidence: high.

**2. Design is for people; you cannot design well without understanding them.**
- "You cannot understand good design if you do not understand people; design is made for people." (S003)
- "Indifference towards people and the reality in which they live is actually the one and only cardinal sin in design." (S007)
- Sources: S003, S007. Confidence: medium.

**3. Aesthetics are part of usefulness, and only good execution is beautiful.**
- "The aesthetic quality of a product is integral to its usefulness because products we use every day affect our person and our well-being." (S001)
- "But only well-executed objects can be beautiful." (S001)
- Sources: S001, S003, S005. Confidence: high.

## Reduction

**4. Less, but better: concentrate on the essential and remove the rest.**
- "What is the main characteristic of your design? Less, but better." (S006)
- "Back to purity, back to simplicity." (S001)
- Sources: S001, S002, S003, S006. Confidence: high.

**5. Simple enough for everyone to understand.**
- "For design to be understood by everyone – which good design should strive to do – it should be as simple as possible." (S003)
- Sources: S001, S003. Confidence: medium.

**6. Decoration to impress is packaging, not design.**
- "To use design to impress, to polish things up, to make them chic, is no design at all. This is packaging." (S003)
- Sources: S003, S009. Confidence: medium.

**7. Quiet beats loud.**
- "The majority of products that we encounter in our day-to-day lives scream for attention or try to impress us with their magnificence or miniscule size." (S003)
- "Their design should therefore be both neutral and restrained, to leave room for the user's self-expression." (S001)
- Sources: S001, S003. Confidence: medium.

## Honesty and detail

**8. Never make a product look more than it is.**
- "It does not make a product more innovative, powerful or valuable than it really is." (S001)
- "they accept innovative solutions – not fake ones" (S003)
- Sources: S001, S003. Confidence: medium.

**9. Nothing arbitrary; detail is respect.**
- "Nothing must be arbitrary or left to chance. Care and accuracy in the design process show respect towards the user." (S001)
- Sources: S001. Confidence: low (one source, but one of the ten principles).

**10. Aesthetic decisions need a reason rooted in purpose.**
- "Their work is largely rational, meaning that aesthetic decisions are justified by an understanding of the product's purpose." (S003)
- Sources: S003, S005. Confidence: medium.

## Business

**11. Good design is a business necessity and often the only visible difference.**
- "in the face of increasing competition, design is often the only product differentiation that is truly discernible to the buyer." (S003)
- "The introduction of good design is needed for a company to be successful." (S003)
- Sources: S003. Confidence: low.

**12. Bad design costs value, not just looks.**
- "A poorly-designed product is not only uglier than a well-designed one but it is of less value and use. Worst of all it might be intrusive." (S003)
- Sources: S003. Confidence: low.

**13. Design must match the company's objectives and is best done inside, with management that understands it.**
- "The ideas behind my work as a designer have to match with a company's objectives." (S003)
- "This is the only way to design useful products, which are not primarily dictated by market orientations or solely profit-oriented marketing." (S006)
- Sources: S003, S006, S002. Confidence: high.

**14. Design is teamwork, not a solo genius act.**
- "first of all, I didn't do anything by myself" (S005)
- "I can only recommend to young designers to be optimistically curious and to see themselves as team players." (S006)
- Sources: S005, S006. Confidence: medium.

## Longevity and waste

**15. Long-lasting over fashionable.**
- "It avoids being fashionable and therefore never appears antiquated." (S001)
- "Unlike fashionable design, it lasts many years – even in today's throwaway society." (S001)
- Sources: S001, S003, S008. Confidence: high.

**16. Waste, physical and visual, is a design failure.**
- "Striving for good design is of social importance as it means, amongst other things, absolutely avoiding waste." (S003)
- "It conserves resources and minimises physical and visual pollution throughout the lifecycle of the product." (S001)
- "There are too many unnecessary products in this world." (S007)
- Sources: S001, S003, S004, S006, S007. Confidence: high.
