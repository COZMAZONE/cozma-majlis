# Sources: Dieter Rams

9 sources, all fetched and read in full on 2026-10-15: one primary speech text (S003), the ten principles as published by Vitsoe under Rams's name (S001), two interviews in Q&A form (S005, S006), one documentary page carrying his quoted lines (S007), and four Vitsoe or press pages for biography and context (S002, S004, S008, S009). About 25,000 words of page text, of which roughly 4,500 words are Rams's own words.

Rams gives few interviews and writes little; his public thought is concentrated in the ten principles, the 1976 speech, and a handful of Q&As. This brain is therefore smaller than a transcript-built brain. Quotes are verbatim from the fetched page text. The designboom interview (S005) is a translated Q&A printed in lower case; quotes keep its wording.

Not used as text: his book *Less but Better* (*Weniger, aber besser*, 1995), the Dezeen/Kinfolk 2017 interview and the Fast Company 2018 interview (both blocked when fetched), and Gary Hustwit's film itself (only the film page S007 was read). Lines that circulate from those sources ("Simplicity is the key to excellence!", "Less would be better everywhere") are not quoted anywhere in this brain.
