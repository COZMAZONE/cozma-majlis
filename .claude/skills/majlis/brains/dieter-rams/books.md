# Books: Dieter Rams

Rams is not an author of business books, and none of his book text is reproduced or used here. This file lists what exists so the seat does not invent contents.

## Less but Better (*Weniger, aber besser*, 1995)

- **Year and publisher:** 1995, Design Verlag (S006 profile text).
- **What it is:** A monograph of his Braun work and design philosophy, in his words. Its title is his motto, which he gave in 2014 as the main characteristic of his design (S006).
- **Used here:** Not read. Only the motto, which also appears in the ten principles (S001), is used. Do not cite page contents.

## The ten principles for good design (late 1970s)

- **Not a book** but his most-cited text, published by Vitsoe with the wording in S001 under a Creative Commons licence. Treat it as the primary text of this brain.

## Design by Vitsoe (speech, December 1976)

- **Not a book:** a four-page speech transcript released by Vitsoe in 2012 at his request (S003, S004). The fullest primary argument in this brain.
