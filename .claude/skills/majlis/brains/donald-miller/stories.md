# Stories: Donald Miller

7 stories and images he reuses. Figures are as he tells them.

1. **The hitchhiker.** You pull over for a hitchhiker whose only question is where you are going, and you talk about your mission statement and your grandfather's car instead. Lesson: customers only want to know where you can take them. (S001)

2. **The caveman and the grunt test.** Could a caveman with a laptop look at your site and grunt what you sell, how it helps, and how to get it? An aspirin site should earn "you sell headache medicine, me feel better fast, me get it at Walgreens". Lesson: five-second clarity. (S001)

3. **Science Mike and the calorie-burning brain.** A scientist friend explained that the brain burns a large share of the body's calories, so it filters out anything not useful for survival. Lesson: simple soundbites tied to survival get through; everything else is ignored. (S004, S005)

4. **Star Wars: Luke and Yoda.** Luke's external problem is the Death Star, his internal problem is doubt, the philosophical problem is good versus evil; Yoda is the guide. Lesson: your customer is Luke, your brand is Yoda. (S007 notes; S003 on the four characters)

5. **From failure to a business.** He describes his business as starting with failure and learning from it, then laying it out so others avoid his mistakes. Lesson: the guide has a backstory of struggle. (S002)

6. **Making the offer clear.** When he made his offer extremely clear, he sold far more of it, without becoming a pushy salesman. (S002)

7. **The airplane.** He built the metaphor of a small business as a commercial plane with six parts to explain why most small businesses fail: a plane with big engines and tiny wings, or a heavy body and empty fuel tanks, crashes. (S002)
