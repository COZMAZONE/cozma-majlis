# Principles: Donald Miller

12 decision rules, clustered across sources. Confidence: high = 3+ sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

## Clarity

**1. Clear beats clever; confusion loses the sale.**
- "If you confuse, you'll lose." (S001)
- "In business, if we don't communicate clearly, we shrink." (S001)
- Sources: S001, S003, S004, S006. Confidence: high.

**2. The fastest-understood product wins, not the best one.**
- "People don't buy the best products; they buy the products they can understand the fastest." (S001)
- "And it doesn't even matter if you have an inferior product." (S005)
- Sources: S001, S005. Confidence: medium.

**3. Connect the product to survival or the brain ignores it.**
- "if I can't associate your product or service with my survival, the brain is actually designed to ignore you." (S005)
- "So your brain is always trying to say, only think about things that will help me survive." (S004)
- Sources: S004, S005, S003. Confidence: high.

**4. Repeat a few soundbites in the same words everywhere.**
- "You need short, simple, memorable, and repeatable sound bites that you can use over and over in the same wording to get people to understand how you can help them survive." (S005)
- Sources: S004, S005, S006. Confidence: high.

## Story

**5. The customer is the hero; the brand is the guide.**
- "Nearly every human being is looking for a guide (or guides) to help them win the day." (S001)
- "every hero needs a guide" (S003)
- Sources: S001, S003, S004. Confidence: high.

**6. Name the problem, especially the internal one.**
- "By talking about the problems our customers face, we deepen their interest in everything we offer." (S001)
- Sources: S001, S003, S004, S007. Confidence: high.

**7. Own one problem and talk about it endlessly.**
- "once you identify what problem you solve, you need to talk about it endlessly because the bigger the problem is, the more valuable you are because you're somebody who solves problems." (S004)
- Sources: S003, S004. Confidence: medium.

**8. Show what is at stake.**
- "Simply put, we must show people the cost of not doing business with us." (S001)
- Sources: S001, S002. Confidence: medium.

**9. Empathy and understanding earn trust.**
- "People trust those who understand them, and they trust brands that understand them too." (S001)
- Sources: S001, S008. Confidence: medium.

## Action

**10. Always call them to action, directly.**
- "CUSTOMERS DO NOT TAKE ACTION UNLESS THEY ARE CHALLENGED TO TAKE ACTION." (S001)
- "Never assume people understand how your brand can change their lives. Tell them." (S001)
- Sources: S001, S002. Confidence: medium.

**11. Words sell, not design.**
- "pretty websites don't sell things. Words sell things." (S001)
- Sources: S001, S002. Confidence: medium.

**12. Keep the six parts of the business in proportion; messaging cannot fix wings or fuel.**
- "Your products are your wings of the airplane." (S002)
- Sources: S002. Confidence: low.
