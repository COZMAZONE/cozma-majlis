# Books: Donald Miller

Built from verbatim book excerpts (S001, Building a StoryBrand), his interviews, and the documented structure of the books. No other book text was fetched; structures not confirmed by a fetched source are paraphrased.

## Building a StoryBrand (2017; Building a StoryBrand 2.0, 2025)

- **Core thesis:** Brands lose because their message is confusing and they cast themselves as the hero. Use the seven elements of story to clarify the message so customers listen, with the customer as hero and the brand as guide.
- **Key frameworks:** SB7 (character, problem, guide, plan, call to action, failure, success); three levels of problem and the villain; guide = empathy + authority; process and agreement plans; direct and transitional calls to action; grunt test; testimonial questions. See frameworks.md #1 to #5, #9.
- **Taught in:** S001 (excerpts), S003, S004, S005 (2.0 era, soundbites).

## Marketing Made Simple (2020, with Dr. J.J. Peterson)

- **Core thesis:** A simple sales funnel built on the StoryBrand message: one-liner, wireframed website, lead generator, nurture emails, sales emails. (Structure paraphrased; not quoted.)
- **Key frameworks:** frameworks.md #6 and #8.

## Hero on a Mission (2022)

- **Core thesis:** Personal application of the four characters (victim, villain, hero, guide); live as a hero and a guide, not a victim or villain. Taught in S003.

## How to Grow Your Small Business (2023)

- **Core thesis:** A small business as an airplane: leadership cockpit, marketing and sales engines, product wings, overhead body, cash-flow fuel; six parts kept in proportion. Taught in S002.

## Earlier

- Memoirs and essays (Blue Like Jazz and others) are his pre-business writing. Do not cite them on marketing.
