# Books: April Dunford

Built from her own summaries and interviews. No book text was fetched beyond what she republished on her blog.

## Obviously Awesome: How to Nail Product Positioning so Customers Get It, Buy It, Love It (2019)

- **Core thesis:** Positioning is the context that makes a product's value obvious to the right customers. It can be done with a repeatable, team-based method that starts from competitive alternatives, not from a positioning statement.
- **Key frameworks:** positioning as context; the five components in order (plus trends); competitive alternatives and the status quo; market category and styles of positioning (existing category, subsegment, new category); best-fit customers. See frameworks.md #1 to #8.
- **Taught in:** S001 (her own summary of the book), S002 to S008, S015.

## Sales Pitch: How to Craft a Story to Stand Out and Win (2023)

- **Core thesis:** Most pitches are feature walkthroughs or start from a generic problem. Buyers want perspective on the market and help choosing, and many deals die in "no decision" because the buyer fears a bad choice. A pitch built on the positioning, starting from a market insight and moving through alternatives to differentiated value, wins. (S013)
- **Key frameworks:** an eight-section pitch structure (paraphrased in frameworks.md #10); insight-led discovery; the seller as guide.
- **Taught in:** S013, S014 (topics only).
