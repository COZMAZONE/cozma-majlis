# Sources: Russell Brunson

14 sources, built 2026-10-15. Eleven are machine transcripts of his own podcast (Marketing Secrets / The Russell Brunson Show) hosted on podscripts.co, two are his own blog and site pages, and one is a ClickFunnels company post. His books (DotCom Secrets, Expert Secrets, Traffic Secrets) were not read in full; they are covered only through how he teaches them on the podcast and through widely documented outlines of their frameworks. No book text is quoted.

Quotes come from auto transcripts, so they keep transcription quirks ("okay?", "right?", repeated words). Where a transcript garbled a word (for example "one offer way" for "one offer away", "hiring coaching" for "higher coaching"), the line is paraphrased, not quoted. Episode descriptions on podscripts were written by his team in his first person; they are cited as "show notes" and quoted sparingly.
