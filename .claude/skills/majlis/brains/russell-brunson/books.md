# Books: Russell Brunson

Built only from how he teaches the books in public (podcast episodes and his own pages) plus their widely documented chapter frameworks. No book text was fetched or is quoted. He describes hook, story, offer as the one framework present in all three books (S001).

## DotCom Secrets (2015; expanded edition 2020)

- **Core thesis:** A business grows online through funnels and a value ladder, not a single product page. Funnels move a dream customer from free or cheap offers up to high-value ones, and a follow-up system with an attractive character keeps them ascending.
- **Key frameworks:** Value Ladder; the secret formula (who is your dream customer, where are they, what bait, what result); attractive character; follow-up funnels (soap opera sequence, daily emails); funnel types (lead, unboxing, presentation, phone). See frameworks.md #6 to #8, #12.
- **Taught in:** S004, S005, S006, S013 (free-plus-shipping relaunch page).

## Expert Secrets (2017; expanded edition 2020)

- **Core thesis:** Turn knowledge into a movement and sell one-to-many with stories that break false beliefs, then a stacked offer.
- **Key frameworks:** Epiphany Bridge story; the stack; the perfect webinar structure; creating a "new opportunity" rather than an improvement offer (documented in the book, paraphrased here).
- **Taught in:** S001.

## Traffic Secrets (2020)

- **Core thesis:** Know your dream customer, find where they already congregate (Dream 100), and fill funnels with traffic you work your way into, buy, or own, so no platform change can kill the business.
- **Key frameworks:** Dream customer; searcher vs scroller; Dream 100; hook, story, offer as the core unit of every ad. See frameworks.md #10 to #11.
- **Taught in:** S009 (book launch series), S010, S002.
- **Figures he cites:** about 36% of buyers taking the audiobook order bump at launch (S009).
