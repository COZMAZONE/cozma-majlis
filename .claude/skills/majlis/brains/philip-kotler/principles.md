# Principles: Philip Kotler

12 decision rules. Confidence: high = 3+ sources or a lifelong textbook position, medium = 2, low = 1. Quotes are verbatim from the fetched text.

## Customer first

**1. Marketing creates value; it does not dispose of what you made.**
- "Marketing is not the art of finding clever ways to dispose of what you make." (S001)
- "Marketing is the art of creating genuine customer value. It is the art of helping your customer become better off." (S001)
- Sources: S001, S003, S005. Confidence: high.

**2. Do the homework first; good marketing makes selling nearly unnecessary.**
- "If the marketer does a good job of identifying consumer needs, developing appropriate products, and pricing, distributing, and promoting them effectively, these goods will sell very easily." (S001)
- "But what he meant was clear: if you create something that truly meets a need, customers will line up for it." (S005, on Drucker)
- Sources: S001, S005. Confidence: high (core textbook position).

## Strategy

**3. Target. Aiming at the average loses.**
- "If you aim for the average, you will lose." (S001)
- "All customers are important but some are more important than others." (S001)
- Sources: S001, S003. Confidence: high.

**4. Win the war on paper before the battle.**
- "You should never go to battle before you’ve won the war on paper." (S001)
- "It is more important to do what is strategically right than what is immediately profitable." (S001)
- Sources: S001. Confidence: medium.

**5. Differentiate; anything can be differentiated.**
- "If you can differentiate a dead chicken, you can differentiate anything." (S001)
- "Competitive advantage is a company’s ability to perform in one or more ways that competitors cannot or will not match." (S001)
- Sources: S001, S003. Confidence: high.

**6. Be the best somewhere, or do not be there.**
- "Your company does not belong in any market where it can’t be the best." (S001)
- Sources: S001. Confidence: low.

## The mix

**7. Use the whole mix, not only promotion.**
- "Marketers influence demand by making the product appropriate, attractive, affordable, and easily available to target consumers." (S001)
- "Hard sell marketing is a contradiction." (S001)
- Sources: S001. Confidence: high (core textbook position).

**8. Price on value, not cost; never compete only on price.**
- "Cost is of no importance in setting the price. It only helps you to know whether you should be making the product." (S001)
- "Then price is everything and the low-cost producer is the only winner." (S001, of a business that is not a brand)
- Sources: S001, S003. Confidence: high.

**9. Under-spending on advertising is the expensive mistake.**
- "If you spend too little on advertising, you are spending too much." (S001)
- Sources: S001. Confidence: low.

## Relationships

**10. Maximize lifetime relationships, not single transactions; delight, do not just satisfy.**
- "It is no longer enough to satisfy your customers. You must delight them." (S001)
- "Try creating customer evangelists, the power of cult branding, and creating raving fans." (S001)
- Sources: S001, S004. Confidence: high.

**11. Measure share of mind and heart, and the path to advocacy.**
- "I don’t care what happened to your profits. Have you improved your share of the customer’s mind and heart this year?" (S001)
- "The role of marketers is to guide customers throughout their journey from awareness to ultimately advocacy." (S004)
- Sources: S001, S004, S007. Confidence: high.

## Change

**12. Markets move faster than marketing; keep re-examining.**
- "Markets always change faster than marketing." (S001)
- "Today you have to run faster to stay in the same place." (S001)
- Sources: S001, S004, S008. Confidence: high.
