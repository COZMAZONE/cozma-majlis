# Books: Philip Kotler

Built from his own publication list (S002), interviews, and public descriptions. No book text is reproduced. He has written or co-written more than 50 books; these are the ones that matter for a store's marketing decisions.

## Marketing Management (1967; 15+ editions; Kevin Lane Keller co-author from 2006; an Arab World edition in 2012)

- **Core thesis:** Marketing is a managerial discipline: analyze markets, choose target markets, position the offer, design the marketing mix, implement and control, to create superior customer value.
- **Key frameworks:** marketing concept; STP; the 4Ps; segmentation bases and criteria; competitive strategies (leader, challenger, follower, nicher); customer lifetime value. See frameworks.md #1 to #7.
- **Taught in:** S001, S003 (definitions from the 1967 and 2001 editions), S009.

## Principles of Marketing (1980; with Gary Armstrong from 1989)

- **Core thesis:** The introductory version of the same system, centred on creating customer value and relationships. Taught in S001, S002.

## Kotler on Marketing (1999) and Marketing Insights from A to Z (2003)

- **Core thesis:** Short essays and aphorisms for managers on targeting, branding, pricing, and service. Source of many lines on S001 and S003 ("No company in its right mind tries to sell to everyone.").

## Marketing 3.0 (2010), 4.0 (2017), 5.0 (2021), 6.0 (2024) (with Hermawan Kartajaya, Iwan Setiawan, and others)

- **Core thesis:** Marketing evolves from product-centric (1.0) to customer-centric (2.0) to human-centric and values-driven (3.0), to blending traditional and digital along the 5A path (4.0), to technology for humanity (5.0), to immersive marketing (6.0).
- **Key frameworks:** 5A path; PAR and BAR; Next Tech; generations. See frameworks.md #8, #9.
- **Taught in:** S004, S007, S008, S002.

## Ten Deadly Marketing Sins (2004), Lateral Marketing (2003), Market Your Way to Growth (2013)
