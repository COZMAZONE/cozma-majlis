# Principles: Bill Bernbach

25 decision rules, clustered across sources. Confidence: high = 3+ sources, medium = 2, low = 1. Quotes are verbatim from the cached sources; lines from quote compilations (S011 to S015) are attributed and usually undated.

## Art over formula

**1. Persuasion is an art. Judge creative work as persuasion, not as compliance with rules.**
- "Advertising is fundamentally persuasion and persuasion happens to be not a science, but an art." (S001)
- "I warn you against believing that advertising is a science." (S008)
- Sources: 12 (S001, S002, S003, S008, S010, S011, S015, S016, S017, S019, S036, S045). Confidence: high.

**2. Hire and reward people who do inspiring things, not people who only do the right things.**
- "I don't want people who do the right things. I want people who do inspiring things." (S001)
- Sources: 3 (S001, S002, S036). Confidence: high.

**3. Rules and formulas produce sameness; break the convention when it serves the product.**
- "As soon as you become a slave to the rules, you're doing what everybody else does" (S024)
- "Rigid rules destroy the creativity so vital to the formation of new ideas to meet new problems." (S003)
- Sources: 9 (S001, S003, S007, S008, S010, S012, S013, S024, S025). Confidence: high.

**4. Technique serves the idea, never the reverse.**
- "Adapt your techniques to an idea, not an idea to your techniques." (S007)
- "Superior technical skill will make a good ad better." (S001)
- Sources: 5 (S001, S007, S013, S014, S039). Confidence: high.

**5. Imitation is suicide; yesterday's winning style is tomorrow's cliche.**
- "in communications, imitation is commercial suicide" (S010)
- "Today's smartest advertising style is tomorrow's corn." (S012)
- Sources: 6 (S003, S010, S012, S013, S038, S035). Confidence: high.

## Attention and freshness

**6. Being noticed is the first job. Unnoticed advertising has failed whatever it says.**
- "If your advertising goes unnoticed, everything else is academic." (S011)
- "Just because your ad looks good is no insurance that it will get looked at." (S011)
- Sources: 6 (S003, S011, S012, S013, S014, S015). Confidence: high.

**7. Be different, or be invisible.**
- "In advertising not to be different is virtually suicidal." (S011)
- "when you do what everybody else does, you don't stand out" (S024)
- Sources: 7 (S011, S013, S015, S024, S025, S035, S039). Confidence: high.

**8. Even a true message goes stale; retell it in a fresh form.**
- "Even a truth must be told in a new, fresh, artful form or it will bore the beholder." (S003)
- "A dull truth will not be looked at." (S003)
- Sources: 4 (S003, S011, S013, S015). Confidence: high.

**9. Impression beats frequency. People remember the one ad that moved them, not how many you ran.**
- "Nobody counts the number of ads you run; they just remember the impression you make." (S011)
- "Properly practiced creativity can make one ad do the work of ten." (S007)
- Sources: 6 (S007, S011, S012, S013, S014, S018). Confidence: high.

## Execution and feeling

**10. How you say it is part of what you say. Great execution becomes content.**
- "Great execution becomes content. And it brings what you have to say to the eyes and ears of your audience believably, persuasively." (S003)
- "It's not just what you say that stirs people. It's the way that you say it." (S012)
- Sources: 6 (S003, S012, S013, S014, S019, S039). Confidence: high.

**11. Facts are not enough. People act on feeling; touch the instinct under the stated reason.**
- "Facts and knowledge are not enough to persuade. As a matter of fact they are often obstacles to persuasion." (S003)
- "nothing is so powerful as an insight into human nature" (S043)
- Sources: 7 (S003, S012, S013, S014, S029, S043, S045). Confidence: high.

**12. Write for what the reader takes away, not for what you put in.**
- "the communicator is concerned not just with what he puts into a piece of writing but with what the reader gets out of it" (S003)
- "the reader reads with his ego, his emotions, with his prejudices, his urges and his aspirations" (S003)
- Sources: 3 (S003, S011, S013). Confidence: high.

**13. Simplify. One theme, nothing that smothers the product message.**
- "Our job is to simplify, to tear away the unrelated, to pluck out the weeds that are smothering the product message." (S011)
- "Sometimes it's better not to put everything into an ad you want to put in." (S003)
- Sources: 7 (S003, S008, S011, S013, S014, S020, S024). Confidence: high.

**14. Imagination must be disciplined by the product theme; graphic acrobatics are not creativity.**
- "The creative person has harnessed his imagination." (S009)
- "The heart of creativity is discipline." (S012)
- Sources: 4 (S009, S012, S013, S027). Confidence: high.

## Truth and the product

**15. Truth is the strongest persuasive material.**
- "The most powerful element in advertising is the truth." (S011)
- Sources: 6 (S011, S013, S014, S023, S035, S039). Confidence: high.

**16. Nobody believes perfection. Admit the flaw to earn belief.**
- "Nobody's perfect and no one's going to believe you if you claim to be." (S005)
- "There are so many of us doing that with our products. But people don't believe us." (S003)
- Sources: 7 (S003, S005, S007, S010, S021, S022, S027). Confidence: high.

**17. Advertising conveys a real advantage; it cannot invent one.**
- "Advertising doesn't create a product advantage. It can only convey it." (S008)
- "you can't invent a product advantage that doesn't exist" (S009)
- Sources: 6 (S008, S009, S011, S013, S014, S035). Confidence: high.

**18. A bad product plus great advertising fails faster.**
- "Great advertising can make a bad product fail faster; it gets more people to know it's bad" (S006)
- "Nothing makes a bad product fail faster than a great advertising campaign" (S006)
- Sources: 6 (S006, S007, S011, S012, S013, S024). Confidence: high.

**19. The product is the hero; kill cleverness that shines brighter than it.**
- "Our job is to kill the cleverness that makes us shine instead of the product." (S011)
- "The magic is in the product" (S009)
- Sources: 6 (S009, S011, S013, S014, S035, S038). Confidence: high.

**20. The purpose of advertising is to sell; art that does not sell is a failure.**
- "The purpose of advertising is to sell." (S005)
- "Properly practiced creativity must result in greater sales more economically achieved." (S011)
- Sources: 5 (S001, S005, S011, S013, S014). Confidence: high.

## Research, judgement and courage

**21. Research is a springboard and a check, not the source of ideas.**
- "A creative man can't jump from nothing to a great idea. He needs a springboard of information." (S003)
- "We don't ask research to do what it was never meant to do, and that is to get an idea." (S014)
- Sources: 7 (S003, S007, S010, S012, S013, S014, S040). Confidence: high.

**22. Do not kill a polarising idea on averages. The half that loves it is the half you want.**
- "and that's the half we want. Let's go with it." (S007)
- "If you stand for something, you will always find some people for you and some against you." (S007)
- Sources: 4 (S007, S012, S013, S014). Confidence: high.

**23. Aim before you fly: talent in the wrong direction just arrives at the wrong place faster.**
- "a great talent, sailing in the wrong direction will, like the lost pilot breaking the speed record, reach the wrong destination more quickly" (S003)
- "Everyman is flawed and therefore everyman needs a check on him." (S003)
- Sources: 1 (S003). Confidence: low.

**24. A principle only counts when it costs you money.**
- "But I don't feel a principle is a principle until it costs you money." (S004)
- "It was a matter of principle and it cost us money" (S004)
- Sources: 6 (S004, S005, S010, S012, S013, S046). Confidence: high.

**25. Courage wins: the energetic and the brave displace the passive.**
- "Those who are going to be in business tomorrow are those who understand that the future, as always, belongs to the brave." (S015)
- "But the energetic displaces the passive." (S011)
- Sources: 4 (S011, S012, S013, S015). Confidence: high.
