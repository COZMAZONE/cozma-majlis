# Sources: Bill Bernbach

49 sources. Bernbach wrote no book, so the corpus is built from what survives of his own words: the 1947 letter to Grey Advertising (S001, S002), the full text of his 1980 speech to the 4A's, "Facts Are Not Enough" (S003, S043, S049), his 1965 and 1971 newspaper interviews as quoted by Barry Popik and Nick Asbury (S004 to S006), his address to the Art Directors Club and his Clio remarks as quoted by the ADC Hall of Fame (S026), the Advertising Hall of Fame and Ad Age Advertising Century profiles (S008, S024), quote compilations that draw on "Bill Bernbach Said..." (DDB, 1989) (S011 to S015), Wikiquote (S016 to S018), and articles on the campaigns (Volkswagen, Avis, Levy's, Alka-Seltzer, Polaroid, Ohrbach's). Critics and biographers are included so the lens can be argued with (S027 to S029, S040, S041).

Quotes in the brain files are verbatim from the cached plain-text copy of each source. Quote-site lines (S011 to S015) usually carry no original date or venue; treat them as attributed, and prefer S001, S003, S004, S006 and S026 when the exact wording matters. Neither "Bill Bernbach Said..." nor "Bill Bernbach's Book" is reproduced; both are described in `books.md` only.
